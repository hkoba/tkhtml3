3. rem vs em

Both bars sit inside a container with font-size 24px. The em bar scales with that container (10em = 240px); the rem bar ignores it and tracks the root font (10rem = 160px).

5. white-space: pre / pre-wrap / pre-line

Same source text in all three boxes: an indented line, a blank line, and one long line. pre clips (no wrapping), pre-wrap keeps the indent and wraps the long line, pre-line collapses the indent but keeps the line breaks and wraps.
